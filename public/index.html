Net equity will calculate from appraised value, ownership, and debt.
Real Estate official value follows net equity. Internal value follows net equity unless an internal override is entered. Appraisal changes do not create IRR cash flows.
Market value and current yield will calculate from par value, price, and coupon.
Fixed Income values sync to official and internal value, but coupon and price fields do not create IRR cash flows.
Cash balances sync to official and internal value, but do not create IRR cash flows.

Market value will calculate from shares and current price.
